Team challenge

Start empty. Beat the reference.

Your starting point

Empty pipeline. Wander mode. Noise 1×, drag 1×. Same as the baseline.

The rule

One stage at a time. Run 60 s. Write down what changed and why before adding the next.

The target

Match or beat the mentor reference's hit rate without pressing Reveal. Then press it and compare.

The reference is deliberately not optimal. If a team beats it, that is a real result and we should adopt their spec. Encourage teams to try a stage nobody built yet.
