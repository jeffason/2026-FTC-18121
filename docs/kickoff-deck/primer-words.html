Shooter 101

Words we will use all season

WordMeans
FlywheelThe spinning wheel that throws the ball.
RPMRevolutions per minute. How fast the flywheel spins.
TargetThe RPM we are asking for. Also called the setpoint.
Spin-upThe time the wheel takes to reach the target after a change or a shot.
ToleranceHow far from the target we still call "close enough" to shoot.
Velocity tableA list of distances and the RPM each one needs. Measured, not guessed.
WordMeans
AprilTagA black and white square marker with an ID number, like a QR code for robots.
LimelightOur smart camera. It finds AprilTags and reports where they are.
RangeDistance from the robot to the goal, in metres.
NoiseSmall random errors in a measurement. Every sensor has some.
DropoutA moment when the camera sees no tags at all.
GateA check that must pass before we shoot. If it fails, we hold.

Metres and RPM everywhere. The code, the sim and this deck all use the same units.

Read these fast, then come back to them as they appear. The two that trip people up are target versus actual RPM, and range versus the robot's position on the field. Range is just how far away the goal is.
