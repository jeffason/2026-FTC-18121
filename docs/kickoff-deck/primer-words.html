Shooter 101

Words we will use all season

WordMeans
FlywheelThe spinning wheel that throws the ball.
RPMRevolutions per minute. How fast the flywheel spins.
TargetThe RPM we are asking for.
Spin-upThe time the wheel takes to reach the target after a change or a shot.
ToleranceHow far from the target still counts as close enough to shoot.
Velocity tableA list of distances and the RPM each one needs. Measured on our robot, not guessed.
NoiseSmall random error in a reading. Every sensor has some.
WordMeans
AprilTagA black and white square marker with an ID number, like a QR code for robots.
LimelightOur smart camera. It finds AprilTags and reports where they are.
RangeDistance from the robot to the goal along the floor, in metres.
DropoutA moment when the camera sees no tags at all.
StageOne small, switchable piece of shooter code with one job.
PipelineOur ordered list of stages. The Limelight also has a setting called pipeline; that one is a camera mode.
SpecThe one-line text that lists the stages and their settings.
TunableA named number inside a stage that you can adjust.

Metres and RPM everywhere. The code, the simulator and this deck use the same words.

You do not need to memorise these now; they come back one at a time. Two of them trip people up: target RPM is what we ask for and actual RPM is what the wheel is doing, and range is simply how far away the goal is.
