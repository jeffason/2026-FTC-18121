Distances in meters, speeds in RPM, everywhere.

You do not need to memorize these now; they come back one at a time tonight. A few need a second sentence. Target RPM is what we ask for; actual RPM is what the wheel is really doing, and tolerance is how far apart those two can be and still shoot. Range is the distance from the robot to the goal along the floor, in meters. An AprilTag is like a QR code for robots, and the Limelight is the camera that finds them. A stage is one small, switchable piece of shooter code with one job; a pipeline is our ordered list of them; a spec is that list written as one line of text; a tunable is a named number inside a stage you can adjust. One warning: the Limelight also has a setting called pipeline, which is a camera mode, not ours.
