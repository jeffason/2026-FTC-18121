We measure the distance, look up the RPM, and shoot. Three things break that on a real field. First, the camera guesses: the tags are only 3.25 inches wide, so in the simulator one tag's range at 5 meters wobbles by about 30 centimeters, and now and then it is simply wrong. Second, the wheel lags: every shot steals speed from the flywheel, and if we feed the next ball too early it leaves slow and falls short. Third, dropouts: something blocks the tags for a moment, and if we moved meanwhile we are aiming at where the goal used to be. An empty pipeline shoots anyway in all three cases. Each problem gets its own small fix, and you build them one at a time: blue stages fix how far, violet stages fix how fast, amber gates decide when.
