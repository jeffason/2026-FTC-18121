A good table is not enough

We measure distance to the goal, look up the flywheel speed that distance needs, and shoot. Three things break that on a real field.

1 · RANGE NOISE

The camera guesses

Tags are 3.25 inches wide. At 5 m a single tag's range wobbles by tens of centimetres, and now and then it is simply wrong.

2 · SPIN-UP

The wheel lags

Every shot steals speed from the flywheel. Feed the next ball too early and it leaves slow and falls short.

3 · DROPOUTS

The goal disappears

A robot or a hand blocks the tags for half a second. If we moved meanwhile, we are aiming at where the goal used to be.

Each problem gets its own fix. That is the whole design.

Ask: what does the baseline do in each case? It shoots anyway. The three colors here are the three kinds of stage you will see on the next slides: blue for range, violet for velocity, amber for gates.
