A good table is not enough

We measure distance to the goal, look up the flywheel speed that distance needs, and shoot. Three things break that on a real field.

1 · RANGE NOISE

The camera guesses

Tags are 3.25 inches wide. In the simulator, one tag's range at 5 m wobbles by about 30 cm, and now and then it is simply wrong.

2 · SPIN-UP

The wheel lags

Every shot steals speed from the flywheel. Feed the next ball too early and it leaves slow and falls short.

3 · DROPOUTS

The goal disappears

Something blocks the tags for a moment. If we moved meanwhile, we are aiming at where the goal used to be.

Each problem gets its own fix. That is the whole design.

An empty pipeline shoots anyway in all three cases. Blue stages fix how far, violet stages fix how fast, amber gates decide when.
