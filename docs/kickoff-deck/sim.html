Open TeamCode/sim/shooter-brain-testbed.html

This is one web page in our code folder; open it from the file, no install. Three brains read the same noisy camera stream: a baseline with an empty pipeline, a hidden mentor-built comparison pipeline, and yours. The bench panel sets how far away the goal is or lets the robot wander, plus camera noise, air drag (air slowing the ball), spin-up time and how often balls are fed. The side view shows the baseline's shots and yours land; a green dot inside the goal opening is a hit, the reference's shots are counted in the stats, and the four squares show which tags the camera saw this frame. Your pipeline is where you add stages from the palette, switch them on and off, drag the sliders and reorder them, and each card shows what it just did and its code. Stats show hit rate, shots per minute, range error and time held for all three brains. Export copies the spec, or the whole Java file, for the robot. Your pipeline is saved in that browser, so it is there next time you open the page. Here it is live: with the Empty pipeline preset your brain and the baseline score about the same. Add TagMedian and watch the range chart tighten. Add VelocityToleranceGate and compare time held and hit rate. The reference stays hidden for now.
