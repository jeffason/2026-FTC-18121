The simulator

One HTML file, no install. Three brains read the same noisy tag stream: a baseline, a hidden mentor-built comparison pipeline, and yours.

PanelWhat you do there
Bench conditionsHold a distance or wander 1 to 5 m. Dial up camera noise, air drag (air slowing the ball), spin-up time, and feeder cadence (how often balls are fed).
Side viewWatch trajectories land. Green dot inside the opening is a hit. Four squares show which tags resolved this frame.
Your pipelineAdd stages from the palette, toggle them, drag sliders, reorder. Each card shows its live input and output and its code.
Stats and chartsHit rate, shots per minute, range error, time held, for all three brains. Range and flywheel over the last 12 s.
ExportCopy the spec, or a complete ShooterConfig.java, into TeamCode.

Open TeamCode/sim/shooter-brain-testbed.html in a browser, straight from the file. Your pipeline is remembered in that browser.

Here it is live. With the Empty preset your brain and the baseline score about the same. Add TagMedian and watch the range chart tighten. Add VelocityToleranceGate and compare time held and hit rate in the stats. The reference stays hidden for now.
