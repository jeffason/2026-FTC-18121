The simulator

One HTML file, no install. Three brains read the same noisy tag stream: a baseline, a hidden mentor reference, and yours.

PanelWhat you do there
Bench conditionsHold a distance or wander 1 to 5 m. Dial up camera noise, air drag, spin-up time, feeder cadence.
Side viewWatch trajectories land. Green dot inside the opening is a hit. Four squares show which tags resolved this frame.
Your pipelineAdd stages from the palette, toggle them, drag sliders, reorder. Each card shows its live input and output and its code.
Stats and chartsHit rate, shots per minute, range error, time held, for all three brains. Range and flywheel over the last 12 s.
ExportCopy the spec, or a complete ShooterConfig.java, into TeamCode.

Open TeamCode/sim/shooter-brain-testbed.html in any browser. Your pipeline is remembered in that browser.

Demo this live if you can. Start with the Empty preset so students see the baseline and your brain match. Add TagMedian and watch the range chart tighten. Add VelocityToleranceGate and watch time held go up while misses go down. Do not reveal the reference yet.
