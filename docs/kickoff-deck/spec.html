The spec: one line that is the whole brain

OutlierReject(maxDevM=0.3); TagMedian; RollingAverage(frames=5);
!FarShotBoost(startM=2.5, pctPerM=2); ManualOffset(offsetRpm=0);
RecentTagGate(maxAgeMs=300); VelocityToleranceGate(tolRpm=60)

Grammar

Semicolons separate stages. Parentheses hold tunables. A leading ! means present but switched off.

Same string, two places

The sim builds it from the stages you add. ShooterConfig.PIPELINE holds it on the robot. Paste either way.

Same math, two places

Each stage is a class in ShooterStages.java and an entry in the sim's DEFS object, with identical names and tunables.

Pipeline.fromSpec() parses it; Pipeline.toSpec() writes it back. A test checks every registered stage round-trips.

Have a student read the spec line aloud and say what it does. That exercise is the whole point of the string: a pipeline you can say in one breath, text in, text out, no screenshots needed to share a result.
