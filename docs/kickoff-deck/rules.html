What to bring back

Your report, one page

  • Your spec string, pasted as text.
  • Hit rate and shots per minute over a 60 s Wander run.
  • One sentence per stage: what it reads, what it changes, what it costs.
  • The miss you could not fix, and your guess why.
  • If you wrote a new stage: its Java class and its test.

How we work

  • Each team makes its own branch from feature/shooter-vision-testbed. Name it after your team.
  • Commit the spec into ShooterConfig and your stages into ShooterStages.
  • Tests must pass before you ask for a review.
  • Open a pull request to merge it back. A mentor and another team review it before it joins.
  • The best pipeline goes on the robot for calibration night.

Questions any time. The README in the shooter package has every command.

Close by scheduling calibration night: the first time the whole stack runs on hardware. Students should leave knowing the date, the branch name, and that their pipeline has a real chance of being the one on the robot.
